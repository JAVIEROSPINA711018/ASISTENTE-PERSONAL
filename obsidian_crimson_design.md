# Cerebro Personal — Design System (Obsidian-Crimson)

## Overview
The design system of Cerebro Personal, customized with a premium structural engineering identity using corporate brand colors: **Azul Técnico `#1F3A52`** (corporate blue) and **Rojo NSR-10 `#901B2F`** (corporate crimson).

## Brand Aesthetics
1. **Architectural Silk (Light Mode):** A clean, crisp background of `#f8fafc` featuring Technical Blue `#1F3A52` and Crimson NSR-10 `#901B2F` as primary pillars.
2. **Cosmic Obsidian (Dark Mode):** An immersive deep navy background of `#070913` with semi-transparent frosted obsidian surfaces (`#0f1423` with glass blur) and neon-electric highlights.
3. **Spring Micro-Interactions:** Custom curve transition physics using `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` that responds organically to user interaction.

## Typography
- **Headlines & Metric Titles:** Geometric `Outfit` font with negative tracking (`-0.03em`) and tight line-heights (`1.15`) representing precision.
- **Reading Content & Labels:** Clean `Inter` font with optimized reading height (`1.55`) for stress-free review of complex reports.

## Neural AI Orb State Machine
- **Idle State:** Breathing purple-blue gradient, floating continuously.
- **Listening State:** Radial orange-red expanding ripples, pulse size mapping user's voice input.
- **Thinking State:** Fast 3D spin rotation with bright cyan and blue emission.
